Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

SubSweep is built so that we hold as little of your data as possible. This page explains what is collected, where it goes, and your choices.

Your subscription list

Everything you add to the tracker (service names, prices, dates, email addresses, the last 4 digits of cards) is saved only in your own browser, using its local storage. It is not sent to our servers, and we cannot see it. If you clear your browser data or switch devices, the list is not recoverable, so keep a copy with "Copy list as CSV".

Receipts you paste are read inside your browser. They are not uploaded.

What we never ask for

  • Access to your email inbox
  • Bank logins, full card numbers or passwords

Your country

To show your currency and local services, the site guesses your country from your device's time zone and language, inside your browser. Nothing is sent to us for this. You can change the country at any time, and your choice is saved in your browser.

Early-access sign-up

If you join early access, your email address and the country selected on the page are sent to us by email through FormSubmit. We use them only to tell you about SubSweep. We don't sell or share them. You can ask us to delete them at any time.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which processes standard connection data (such as IP address) to deliver the site, under GitHub's privacy statement. We don't use analytics or tracking scripts.

Cookies

SubSweep doesn't set advertising or tracking cookies. The tracker uses your browser's local storage only to keep your list and settings.

Links to other sites

"How to cancel", Gmail search and calendar links open other services (such as Google). Their own privacy policies apply there.

Your rights and contact

You can delete your tracker data any time with "Delete all" or by clearing your browser data. For questions or to delete your early-access sign-up, contact us at the address in our early-access emails.

This policy is written in plain language and should be reviewed by a qualified adviser for your jurisdiction (including the UAE Personal Data Protection Law) before relying on it.
