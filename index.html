Questions

Do you read my email?

Only if you click "Connect Gmail", and then only inside your own browser, with read-only access that's removed right after the scan. Nothing is sent to our servers. Prefer not to connect? Use the ready-made searches and paste receipts yourself.

Where is my data stored?

In your browser's storage on this device. Clearing your browser data removes it, so use "Copy list as CSV" to keep a backup.

Can SubSweep cancel subscriptions for me?

Not automatically. That would need your passwords. Instead, we link you to the right cancel page and remind you before you're charged.

I was charged after I cancelled. What can I do?

Keep your cancellation email, contact the merchant first, then your bank's dispute team. This is general information, not legal advice.

Does it work in my country?

Yes. SubSweep picks your currency and popular local services from your device's time zone. You can change the country at the top of the page.
